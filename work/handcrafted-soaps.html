Handcrafted Soaps

Six cold-process soaps with one-word moods, and two creative routes explored side by side before launch.

Discipline

Product range launch

For

Isha Life

Deliverables

Naming, photo direction, two ad routes, launch page, emailer

1Idea

Name the feeling, not the ingredient.

Each bar got a one-word mood (Detox, Glow, Heal, Soothe, Calm and Cleanse) with the botanicals as the supporting line: Activated Charcoal with Black Pepper, Avarampoo with Basil, Rose Geranium with Coconut Oil. The tone: premium yet affordable, with handmade warmth.

2Image

Two routes, one range.

Before launch, two creative routes were built in full. Simple keeps the bar and box in a clean, airy set. Creative adds texture, hands, jute and crumpled paper for a crafted feel. Seeing them side by side made the choice clear for each channel.

Ritual, in motion.

A film for Arappu, the ancient leaf for a gentle cleanse, and a launch film introducing the full range of handcrafted bathing bars.

Role on these films: strategy and part of the direction.

4System

A launch kit from one shoot.

A single photography direction fed the launch page, the emailer and a social series, so every touchpoint felt like the same handmade range.
