Work/Brand refresh

Isha Vidhya

Refreshing a rural education initiative so it feels as playful as its classrooms and as trustworthy as a CSR proposal, then carrying it into a new donation-led website.

Discipline

Brand refresh, web design

For

Isha Vidhya, Educating Rural India

Deliverables

Identity, website, social, mailers, stationery

1Idea

Return the identity to its purpose.

Isha Vidhya brings quality, English-medium education to rural children. The refresh had five goals: bring in playfulness and youthfulness, keep a simple but formal look for CSR pitches, encourage donations, present the school as an energising place, and stay close to the original colours for familiarity.

2Image

Warm, bright, and grown-up.

A logo suite for every surface, a warm orange and deep maroon palette, and illustrated patterns drawn from the classroom: pencils, books, footballs and tiffin boxes. The same kit carries onto stationery and school signage.

Patterns
3System

Every touchpoint asks to help.

A donation-led website, a social series built on real student stories, a welcome carousel in English and Tamil, and donor emailers for direct giving and CSR partnerships.

4Website

A website that asks you to help.

The next step was a new website built on the refreshed identity: a home page, an Our Schools page and a Volunteer page, designed for desktop, tablet and phone. Real numbers come first (9 schools, 9,000+ students, a 99.7% Class 12 pass rate), then the classrooms, the students’ own stories and a clear way to give, whether you are a parent, a volunteer or a company’s CSR team.

Give in under a minute

Pick one-time, yearly or monthly, choose a fund, set the number of children. Or send a ready-to-pay gift link to a friend.

Built for CSR teams

Approvals, audited reporting, recognition and a downloadable profile, all in one section.

Phone first

A sticky Volunteer and Sponsor bar on mobile, so the next step is always one tap away.
