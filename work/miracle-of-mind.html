Work/Digital campaign

Miracle of Mind

A meditation app campaign in two moods: tactile paper collage for people new to meditation, and a night sky for the rewards store.

Discipline

Digital campaign, art direction

For

Miracle of Mind app

Formats

Portrait social, 1080 × 1440

1Idea

Meet the restless mind where it is.

The collage series speaks to people who find meditation hard. Real faces are surrounded by hand-drawn alarms, rockets and scribbles, with honest lines like “Why meditation feels difficult at first”.

2Image

Then reward the habit.

A second mood for the rewards store launch: dark, cinematic and quiet, with coins as the hero object and red serif italics carrying the message.

3System

Small stories that sell a feature.

Short sequences turn features into moments people recognise: a prompt to try with an AI assistant, or a missed call from Mom that leads to a gift.
