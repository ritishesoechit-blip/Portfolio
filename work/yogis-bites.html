Work/Range launch

Yogi's Bites

A new range of healthy snacks launched with one visual system, with room for each flavour to have its own character.

Discipline

Range launch, packaging, campaign

For

Isha Life

Deliverables

Art direction sheet, packs, key visuals, landing page, ads, print

1Idea

Snacking, without the guilt.

Snacking is often a guilty pleasure. The range answered with nutrient-dense mixes rooted in yogic wisdom, and the art direction sheet set the rules before any layout: a colour per flavour, a condensed headline face with a friendly script accent, and a library of illustrated ingredients.

2Image

Packs that read at a glance.

Front-of-pack designs, ingredient stories and claims panels built for a phone screen first, with photography that puts families and real moments at the centre.

3Film

Three films, three appetites.

A trail mix for the hiking crowd, a breakfast mix of berries, nuts and seeds, and a brand film that ties the range back to its roots.

Role on these films: strategy and part of the direction.

4System

Teaser, launch, everywhere.

A teaser built curiosity, then the launch rolled out across web and mobile banners, a landing page, social ads, a carousel and a printed cloth banner, all from the same kit.
